# Changelog

## v0.10.0 — public keyless IVS limited beta

- Real, frozen public SEC metadata → fictional portfolio/Thesis → scripted Fast
  and explicit same-task Deep revision → cited evidence → Decimal account What-if.
- Visible real-public / synthetic / scripted labels, no keys or runtime provider
  requests. Missing evidence remains UNDETERMINED; missing prices remain unknown.
- Completed research assertions are strict. Research saves retain a write-scoped
  WAL connection; repeated same-database and failure-path checks guard this path.
- Public Quickstart, three screenshots, import/resource closure and actual
  result-card What-if browser verification. The earlier read-only fictional
  replay and SEC CLI remain available; no live investment-quality claim is made.

## v0.9.0 — limited beta

- **Decision-first research page.** Opens on the Action with up to four distinct reasons, the strongest counterevidence, the change condition and the thesis delta, each shown once; repeated citations of one source are disclosed as one source.
- **Single-account What-if** on a saved Action: explicit shares, hypothetical fill and fees → cash, shares, NAV, weights and concentration, with the trade itself, repricing and modeled P&L separated in `Decimal`.
- **Synthetic technical conditions.** Qualified regular-session OHLCV → SMA20, simple ATR14 and 10-session candidate bands, with a hypothetical account preview.
- **Same-task Fast → Deep continuation**, historical trailing-twelve-month figures in Deep, per-symbol Today coverage, and local price-scenario receipts from your own dated prices.
